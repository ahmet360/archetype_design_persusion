## Overview


The Outlaw archetype is driven by a desire for **freedom from convention**. Outlaws want to live by their own rules, and they reject the expectations that society, authority or the mainstream place on them. They see conformity as a trap. Outlaw brands promise their audience permission to break away, be themselves and stand apart from the crowd.


## Key characteristics


- **Rebellious and defiant:** Outlaw brands push back against authority and accepted norms. They question the way things are usually done and refuse to play it safe.
- **Unapologetically bold:** They are fearless and confident about being different. They don't soften their message to please everyone.
- **Tribal and loyal:** They build a fierce community of people who share the same outsider spirit. Belonging to the brand feels like joining a group of fellow rebels.


You can often spot an Outlaw brand by its **rule-breaking design**. It deliberately ignores the conventions of its category, so it looks and sounds nothing like its competitors.


## Brand or website example


**[Harley-Davidson](https://www.harley-davidson.com/)**


Harley-Davidson is a clear example of the Outlaw archetype. Its website gives a prominent place to the **rider community**, especially the [Harley Owners Group (H.O.G.)](https://www.harley-davidson.com/us/en/content/hog.html), along with rallies, rides and owner events. Buying a Harley is presented as joining a tribe of riders who live on their own terms, not just as buying a motorcycle. This sense of loyal, outsider belonging is central to the brand's rebellious identity.


## Application to web design


- **Layout:** Break from safe, predictable templates. Use asymmetric compositions, overlapping elements, bold full-width sections and unexpected grid choices so the site feels different from typical competitors in its category.
- **Color:** Use a dark, high-contrast palette, such as black, charcoal and gunmetal, with sharp accents like deep red, orange or chrome. Dark tones feel raw and defiant, and they set the brand apart from bright, polished mainstream sites.
